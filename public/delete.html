Delete your 총칼대전 account

In the app

  1. Open the app and tap your profile at the top of the home screen.
  2. Tap [Delete account] at the bottom.
  3. Tap [Delete] to confirm. Google accounts confirm the Google sign-in once more.

If you cannot use the app

  • Sign in with the same Google account on the web version at onecue-lac.vercel.app and delete it the same way. Guest accounts can only be deleted on the device they were created on.
  • Or email dnjsrb980607@gmail.com with your nickname and the Google email you signed in with. We will delete the account and reply within 7 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Google link or guest ID)
  • Nickname, character color and profile character
  • Scores, match history and win streaks per mode, and your leaderboard entries
  • Tickets, coins, quest progress, mailbox, owned skins and arenas, Remove Ads, AI mode progress (including items bought with money — this cannot be undone)
  • Purchase records (order number, product, purchase time)
  • Friends, friend requests and room invites (including entries in other players' lists)

What is kept

  • Nothing in the Game. Past opponents keep their own win/loss counts, but your name and account are not kept there.
  • Google Play keeps its own payment records under Google's policies.
  • The advertising ID belongs to your device, not your game account. Reset or delete it in Android Settings → Security and privacy → Other privacy settings → Ads (the path may differ by device).

See the Privacy Policy for details.
